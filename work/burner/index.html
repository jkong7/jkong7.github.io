A second life that doesn't pause

I built Burner as a simulated iPhone with a second life inside it. The characters text first and sometimes leave me on read. They post, they call, and they remember.

The point is that their lives keep moving while I'm offline. What happened while I was away is part of the world, not a gap in it.

The hard part is the world clock

The hard part isn't the models. It's the clock. Events run on a server-side schedule, so the sim doesn't pause when I close the app.

That one decision shapes everything else. A character who texts while I'm away is living on the world's time, not mine. Catching up means seeing what I missed, not restarting.
